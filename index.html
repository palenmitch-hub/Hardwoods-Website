About the Shop

Mitch's Hardwoods is a one-man custom woodworking shop based in Woodstock, Georgia. Every cutting board and Adirondack chair is handcrafted from premium hardwoods, built to last for generations.

Whether you're looking for a beautiful end-grain board for your kitchen or a comfortable chair for your porch, each piece is made with the same attention to detail and love of the craft.
